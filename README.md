# JET OFF

**Turn aspiration into real experience.**

JET OFF is a lifestyle game that helps people practise the mindset, skills, and behaviours of the life they want — then gives them structured ways to step into pieces of that life in the real world.

---

## What is this?

This is the playable pilot prototype of JET OFF.

It combines:
- Mission-based challenges
- Character growth (Mindset, Presence, Discipline, Network)
- Real-life preparation (routes, etiquette, venue knowledge)
- A clear path toward the ultimate milestone: **Jet Off**

### Current Features (v1.0)

- Interactive missions with objectives
- Live XP and level system
- Guaranteed Level-Up on completing the full pilot loop
- Character stats that grow as you play
- Daily challenges
- Virtual tour + routes + etiquette training
- Real Visit + Photo Share mission
- Final Jet Off vision (Stage 10)

---

## How to Play

1. Open the app
2. Go to **Missions**
3. Complete the objectives in Mission 1
4. Claim the reward to unlock Mission 2
5. Continue through Mission 3 (The Real Visit)
6. Watch your stats and level grow

No contribution is too small. We stand with you.

---

## Pilot Loop

| Mission | Name                  | Focus                          |
|---------|-----------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1       | The Aspiration        | Decision + first commitment    |
| 2       | Know Before You Go    | Tour, routes, etiquette        |
| 3       | The Real Visit        | Real experience + photo share  |

Completing Mission 3 gives a **guaranteed Level-Up**.
